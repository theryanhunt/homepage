# New Kanagawa Color Themes

Spent some time today adding proper theming to the site. I've been a fan of the [Kanagawa](https://github.com/rebelot/kanagawa.nvim) color palette for a while now—it's what I use in my editor—so I figured I'd bring all three variants here.

## The options

- **Wave** — The default. Cool, muted blues with that classic dark mode feel.
- **Dragon** — Warmer. Reds and oranges. Good for late nights.
- **Lotus** — Light mode, but not eye-searing. Warm cream background with purple accents.

Click the little `*` in the nav to switch between them. Your choice persists across visits.

## Why bother?

Honestly? I just wanted the option. Sometimes I'm in the mood for something warmer, sometimes lighter. And it gave me an excuse to clean up the CSS variables and add a bit more breathing room to the layout while I was at it.

Nothing groundbreaking. Just a small quality-of-life thing.
