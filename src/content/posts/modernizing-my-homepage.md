# Modernizing My Homepage

I’ve kept my personal site intentionally lean, but it’s time to refresh it. The goal isn’t to overbuild; it’s to make it easier to keep content current and to highlight the work I care about most.

## What I’m optimizing for

- A clean, fast reading experience
- A content workflow I can maintain
- A structure that scales with new ideas

## What’s next

I’m migrating posts into Markdown, improving the writing flow, and keeping the UI minimal. The focus remains on clarity and signal over noise.
